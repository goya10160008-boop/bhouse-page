House rules

The main points are below. Tap a title to read the full rules.

  • No curfew
  • Quiet hours: 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM
  • Visitors welcome from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM
Visitors
  • Visitors are allowed only from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
  • Tenants are responsible for the behavior of their visitors.
  • Overnight guests are permitted. Guests who stay often, or for more than three (3) nights in a row, may be counted as additional occupants and may be charged extra.
  • Management may refuse visitors or overnight stays when necessary.
General
  • Respect fellow boarders, visitors and staff.
  • Keep noise low, especially from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
  • Keep rooms and common areas clean.
  • Dispose of garbage properly.
  • Tenants pay for any damages they cause.
Fire safety
  • No smoking or cooking inside rooms.
  • Use the designated cooking area only.
  • Do not leave appliances unattended. Unplug them when not in use.
  • No overloading outlets, open flames or unsafe devices.
Electricity and appliances
  • Switch off lights and appliances when leaving.
  • High-power appliances (stoves, heaters, induction cookers) need approval.
  • Rice cookers and other cooking appliances are for the cooking area only.
  • No unauthorized wiring or electrical tampering, including light bulbs.
  • No homemade or improvised extension cords. Only commercially made extension cords may be used.
  • Tenants are liable for misuse. Management may inspect for safety.
Cleanliness and common areas
  • Clean the cooking area after use.
  • Keep bathrooms and shared spaces clean.
  • No personal items in hallways or common areas.
Security and safety
  • Lock your room when leaving.
  • Report suspicious activity.
  • No illegal acts, drinking sessions or gambling.

Violations may result in warnings, fines, or termination of boarding privileges.
